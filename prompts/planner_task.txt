Base the plan on the specific visual details of this vessel, not on general assumptions about vessels in this condition.
Before the plan, decide and commit to one reading of the casualty and one course of action, in three lines:
Casualty type: [aground / capsized / sunken / on fire - choose one]
Observed conditions: [what in the image shows it; write "cannot tell from the image" for anything the image does not show]
Course of action: [the one method you will use, and the visible feature that justifies it]
Then write "Salvage Plan:" and the plan for that course of action only, as a numbered sequence of at most 15 steps in the order they should be carried out.
Each step should state what vessel, resource, or response crew is used to perform it, referring to each asset by its ledger ID. Name only the assets the plan uses.
Commit to a single course of action: write the one plan you will carry out, not a set of options to choose from later. Every step is an action that will happen, and no step depends on how an earlier step turns out. So do not write conditional steps, backup or reserve steps, steps that wait to see whether an earlier step worked, or alternatives.
Include any assumptions, limitations, or requests alongside the steps.

Write one step per line, beginning with step 1. No preamble before the three lines, no markdown tables, and do not restate the ledger.
